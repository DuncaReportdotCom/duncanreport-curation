About DuncanReport.com

DuncanReport.com is an independent, AI-assisted news aggregator. Several times a day it scans a wide range of news outlets across the political spectrum, selects the stories that matter, and organizes them into a fast, single-screen front page in the tradition of the classic news-link sites.

Our mission

Most people read the news through a single lens. Our goal is the opposite: to put left-leaning, centrist, and right-leaning outlets side by side so readers can see the full picture and judge for themselves. The source bar at the top of each page deliberately mixes outlets from across the spectrum — that balance is the point.

How it works

An automated curation engine crawls each section's own set of news sources, ranks and clusters the day's stories, and builds the page. Every headline links directly to the original article on the publisher's own website — we send readers to the source, we don't republish their content. Stories rotate through a rolling multi-day window so developing storylines stay visible.

Our sections

The site is organized into six pages, each with its own sources and focus: Main, Sports, World, Markets, Politics, and Life & Culture.

Who runs it

DuncanReport.com is owned and operated by Duncan Report LLC. It is an independent project, not affiliated with any news outlet, political party, or campaign.
