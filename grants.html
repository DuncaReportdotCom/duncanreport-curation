Investigative Journalism Grants

Duncan Report is paying grants to independent journalists to fund the production of studies and articles relating to major issues facing our country.

Our interest is in funding issues of national importance. Those issues include, but are not limited to, the following:

  • Government spending, waste, fraud, and accountability
  • The integrity and administration of elections
  • Public safety and the justice system
  • Immigration and border security
  • The cost, quality, and access of health care
  • Education, from K–12 through the universities
  • Energy and the reliability of the power grid
  • National security and defense
  • Civil liberties, free speech, and due process
  • Big Tech, artificial intelligence, and data privacy
  • The transparency and accountability of government at every level

We have a particular interest in funding FOIA requests and data analysis. While the aim is to help independent journalists grow their footprint and reputation, we do ask for right of first publication.

Duncan Report will do our best to, at a minimum, speak directly with any applicant — because if you take the time to apply, we want to acknowledge your effort. This isn't a blind email.

Grant amounts
Initial research: $2,500  ·  Fully investigated topics: $10,000
The amount may change depending on the potential importance of the story, and Duncan Report may also provide other forms of support.

Application

Fields marked * are required. Submitting opens a pre-addressed email to our grants desk with your answers; you can also email the same information directly to contact@duncanreport.com.

By applying you acknowledge that Duncan Report requests right of first publication for work it funds. Applying does not guarantee a grant.
